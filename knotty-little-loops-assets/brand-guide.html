02

Logo system

The logo is the strongest visual signature. Preserve its handmade yarn character and circular badge structure.

Primary logo

Use the complete circular logo for the website, packaging, stickers, thank-you cards, social profiles and business collateral.

Clear space

Keep at least ½ the height of the “K” as free space around the logo. Never let product photography, text or UI elements touch the mark.

04

Typography

The logo has its own yarn lettering. Website typography should support it, not compete with it.

Primary UI & body · Nunito Sans The little things matter. Aa Bb Cc · 0123456789

Navigation, body copy, product details, buttons, forms and utility text. A contemporary, highly usable sans family with multiple weights and variable-font support.

Display accent · Fredoka Little things. Big smiles. Aa Bb Cc · 0123456789

Use selectively for playful collection headings, campaign statements and youth-oriented moments. A rounded display sans with multiple weights and an open-source licence.

06

Photography & imagery

The product is the decoration.

Product photography

Warm white background · soft natural shadows · close-up texture · minimal props · warm daylight · honest materials.

Lifestyle photography

Show products on bags, backpacks, desks, bookshelves and in hands. Create situations that help the viewer imagine ownership.

Making-of photography

Show yarn, hooks, hands, unfinished pieces, loose threads, packaging and finished products. This is where craftsmanship becomes visible.

07

Illustration & graphic language

Build a small visual vocabulary around the physical act of crochet.

Yarn loops

Abstract loops can become section dividers, borders and subtle patterns.

Yarn balls

Use as small category or campaign motifs.

Crochet hooks

Use sparingly as a signature craft cue.

Stitch lines

Dashed or stitched rules echo the logo border.

Leaves

A restrained natural motif for seasonal or calm collections.

Character details

Let products supply most of the visual personality.

08

Digital / UI design system

The website should feel like a calm handmade shop, not a loud children's marketplace.

Buttons

Rounded, but not extreme pills. Primary: Brown + white. Hover: Blush + Brown.

Cards

12–20 px radius, subtle border, minimal shadow, warm-white surfaces.

Icons

Rounded line icons. Avoid heavy 3D, sharp geometric icons and excessive emoji.

Forms

Clear labels, generous touch targets, visible focus state and simple error language.

Accessibility & guardrails

Readable, inclusive and consistent execution.

  • Use strong brown text for important information; do not use pale pink as body text.
  • Aim for 16 px or larger body copy on the website.
  • Provide descriptive alt text for every product image.
  • Do not rely on colour alone to communicate availability, category or state.
  • Maintain visible keyboard focus states and comfortable touch targets.
  • Keep product names, prices and actions unambiguous.
DoDon't
Use warm white + brown as the base.Make everything pink.
Let crochet texture be the hero.Decorate every surface with hearts/stars.
Use playful moments selectively.Make the whole site look like a children's toy store.
Show real hands and making.Use overly polished generic stock imagery.
Keep copy short and human.Use corporate marketing language.
Use consistent tokens.Invent a new colour for every product.
